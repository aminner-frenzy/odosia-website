How it works

One city, one list, one trusted source.

Odosia brings together the recommendations you trust most — your friends, your favorite people, and the places they’ve actually loved. Instead of digging through endless group chats, you get a clean, city-by-city guide that feels like it was built by people who already know the place.

Save the recs

Save a restaurant, hotel, bar, or other spot as soon as you hear about it. The app keeps it in the right city so it is ready when you travel.

Follow the right people

See which friends have been to a place, what they loved, and which recs they keep coming back to. No noise, just the signals that matter.

Share in a tap

Send a city guide to a friend before they travel. Everything you’ve loved there is ready to go in one neat package.

Built around trust

People over public rankings

  • Saved recommendations stay attached to the city and the person who sent them.
  • Lists can be private, shared with friends, or sent to a specific trip.
  • Every rec gets context: who recommended it, what they loved, and where it fits in the trip.
Why it feels different

It’s not “best in town.” It’s “best for us.”

Odosia turns scattered recommendations into a city guide built from the people you trust, so you can follow a route without starting from zero.
